# ABU UI/UX Talent Hunt 2026 – Challenge 1 Submission

## Project Overview
A lightweight, responsive, low-bandwidth University Website for Enhanced Academic and Research Discovery.

## How to Run Locally
1. Extract this ZIP archive.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
3. No internet connection, local server, or external dependencies are required.

## Key Features Demonstrated
- **Global Search:** Instant client-side filtering across programmes, research topics, and scholars.
- **Featured Scholars:** Researcher profiles with publication counts and collaboration links.
- **Academic Calendar & Announcements:** Scannable milestones and centralized university notices.
- **Financial Information:** Interactive Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) fee schedules.
- **General Support & AI Assistant:** Interactive ticket form and rule-based discovery chatbot.

## Compliance & AI Disclosure
- All names, fees, and research figures are strictly fictitious demonstration data.
- Initial HTML/CSS/JS scaffolding was generated with AI assistance and customized by the participant.
